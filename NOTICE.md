# Third-party material

- **`data/source_speech.src`** — clips from **Multilingual LibriSpeech (MLS), Portuguese** (Pratap et al., 2020, “MLS: A Large-Scale Multilingual Dataset for Speech Research”), distributed at <https://huggingface.co/datasets/facebook/multilingual_librispeech> under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0** (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>). The recordings are LibriVox public-domain audiobook readings. Clips were trimmed to 10 s and resampled to 16 kHz; sustained vowels were resynthesised from stable frames with the WORLD vocoder. Pitch tracks: WORLD Harvest.
- **RVC exporter classes** — not included; downloaded on first run from [w-okada/voice-changer](https://github.com/w-okada/voice-changer) at commit `d8ef15799470193f7c8176ef471245753a656626`, under its [MIT licence](https://github.com/w-okada/voice-changer/blob/d8ef15799470193f7c8176ef471245753a656626/LICENSE) and [LICENSE-NOTICE](https://github.com/w-okada/voice-changer/blob/d8ef15799470193f7c8176ef471245753a656626/LICENSE-NOTICE).
- **ContentVec encoder** — not included; downloaded on first run from <https://huggingface.co/wok000/vcclient_modules> (`contentvec/contentvec-f.onnx`, SHA-256 `4b31ed3d…3156db`). See that repository for its terms.
- Voice models you convert keep their own licences.
